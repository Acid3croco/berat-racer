# Bérat (31370) low-poly style guide

Method: Claude-in-Chrome, real screenshots of Wikimedia Commons originals (1400-1920 px thumbs) and Bing Images thumbnails. Colours are eyeballed from screenshots (JPEG, varying light), so treat every RGB/hex as +/-15.

Confidence tags: [SEEN] = clearly visible in a photo I viewed; [THUMB] = seen only in a small/low-res thumbnail; [GENERIC] = general Toulouse-area knowledge, NOT verified for Bérat; [NOT SEEN] = no usable image found.

## 1. House types

| Type | Share (my estimate) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Village-centre 2-storey public/civic and merchant blocks around the square | ~15% | [SEEN] mairie, post office, pharmacie, church-side ochre building |
| Detached pavilion houses, 1-2 storeys, on a plot, mostly modern/recent | ~55% | [THUMB] cream/white render, brick surround accents, hipped low roof, garage |
| Old brick farmhouses / maisons de maitre, rural edge | ~20% | [THUMB] exposed red brick, blue shutters, low roof, iron railing at front |
| New-build white/grey-render boxes with garage | ~10% | [THUMB] low-slope terracotta roof, dark grey joinery, grey block walls |

Shares are my judgement from a handful of images, not a survey.

### Roofs
- Material: canal/"tige de botte" terracotta half-round tiles, always. [SEEN]
- Pitch: LOW, about 15-25 deg (roughly 30-40% slope). Hipped roofs on mairie and pharmacie; gable roofs on the old brick post office (gable end faces the street, with brick pediment) and on farmhouses. [SEEN]
- Eaves: wide, brick-corbelled or timber under-eave on old buildings. Grey zinc gutters and a plain round grey zinc downpipe on one face. [SEEN on post office and mairie]
- Colour: weathered orange-brown, mixed: new tiles ~ #C8683C, aged ~ #A5583A, dark/lichened ~ #7A4A3A. [SEEN]
- Chimneys: small square brick or rendered stack on ridge, 1 per house. [THUMB]

### Walls (share of all wall surface)
- Exposed Toulouse brick ("brique foraine", thin, irregular, red-orange with paler mortar): ~30% (church nave, post office, farmhouses). ~#B5654A body, mortar ~#C9B29A. [SEEN]
- Pink/cream/beige crepi render: ~40%. Cream ~#E8DCC8, warm pink ~#D9A98F, off-white ~#EFEAE0. [SEEN mairie, church, pharmacie; THUMB houses]
- Ochre/yellow-brown stone or dressed brick, muted: ~5% (long building beside the church, seen in winter light). ~#B79A6A. [SEEN, one photo, flat grey light]
- Pebble/galets (herringbone galets + brick courses): ~3%, walls/barns only. [GENERIC, a Bing thumbnail titled "Briques et galets" seen but not Bérat-confirmed]
- White or light grey modern render: ~20%. ~#F2F0EA / #CFCBC4. [THUMB]
- Half-timber: not seen in Bérat. Do not use. [GENERIC for wider region]

### Trim
- Brick surrounds / brick string courses and corbels around windows, doors, arches on rendered facades: THE signature detail. Orange ~#C8683C. [SEEN mairie, church, houses]
- Pale limestone/ceramic blocks at window corners (chainage) on old brick building: ~#D8D2C6. [SEEN post office]

### Shutters (volets) and doors
- Light blue / periwinkle is dominant in the old core: ~#7FA0D8 (post office), ~#8FA6D0 (church-side building), ~#7DA3C8 (farmhouse). [SEEN]
- Other seen: pale grey-white shutters on a cream house ~#DCDAD4 [THUMB]; teal/green shutters on a brick house next to the pharmacie ~#4E8F86 [THUMB].
- Modern roller shutters (white or dark grey), dark-grey window frames on the mairie ~#3A3D42. [SEEN]
- Doors: varnished wood (honey ~#B9803F) in brick arches [SEEN mairie and church door thumb]; light-blue painted on farmhouse [THUMB].

### Fences, walls, hedges
- Low brick pier + iron railing (dark green/black) in front of old farmhouse. [THUMB]
- Rendered low wall with metal gate, or grey block wall with a gate. [THUMB]
- Green wire mesh fence (~#3F7A55) beside church and cemetery. [SEEN]
- Cypress / tall conifers lining plots. [THUMB]

## 2. Landmarks

### Eglise Saint-Pierre [SEEN, 4 photos]
- Tower: freestanding-looking, tall, slender OCTAGONAL bell tower, ~4-5 times nave-ridge height. White/cream render shafts with 3 stacked tiers of paired round-arched openings; each tier framed by orange brick arches and horizontal brick bands. Crowned by a corbelled brick cornice and a stone balustrade (NO spire, flat top with balustrade). Small round clock face on the upper tier. Slit belfry openings with dark louvres.
- Colours: shaft ~#E9E4DA, brick bands ~#B5583A; one thumb in grey winter light showed the tower looking ochre/sandy (#B79A6A), so render weathers between white and buff.
- Nave: single wide low-pitched gabled nave, brick buttresses at the corners and mid-wall (brique foraine ~#B5654A), rendered infill panels in cream/grey ~#D8CFBE; big plain rendered gable (the east end seen is flat, gable ~#C9C2B4). Tile roof ~#A5583A, pitch ~20 deg. Lean-to aisles/side chapels rendered cream, tile roof.
- Apse: polygonal, brick buttresses between cream-render panels; tall lancet windows with orange brick arch surrounds; lower base is dirty cream.
- West door: orange brick arch surround, wood door (from thumbnail only, not examined at full size).
- No clocher-mur (bell wall) here: it is a tower. Brick "Toulouse-Gothic" style.
- Setting: sits on the main square, cemetery to the side (grey/black granite tombs, bright flowers), plane/deciduous trees nearby.

### Mairie (1 place de la Mairie) [SEEN, 1 photo]
- 2 storeys plus hipped terracotta roof (low pitch, ~20 deg) with a dark louvred/slatted attic band under the eaves.
- Ground floor: 5-6 wide round brick arches (rows of brick voussoirs) forming an open arcade, now glazed with dark roller shutters, giving a market-hall look. Central door in a brick arch, honey wooden doors, small wrought-iron balcony above.
- First floor: white/cream render panels framed by orange brick pilasters and string course; tall rectangular windows with brick surrounds and dark-grey frames. "MAIRIE" in dark metal letters. EU/French flags.
- Colours: render #EDEAE2, brick #C8683C, roof #C56A45, red planters #C4262E. Pavement: gravelly beige with a terracotta line.
- Note: recent renovation, so crisp and clean. This is the best template for a "civic building with arcade" and doubles as the market-hall/marketplace look (see below).

### Post office (La Poste) [SEEN, 1 photo]
- 2-storey narrow gabled building, exposed thin red brick, pediment gable with brick coping and small arched attic vent. Painted-lettering panels "TELEGRAPHE / POSTES TELEPHONE / BERAT" in blue-grey enamel. Pale-limestone corner blocks at windows, light-blue shutters, honey wood door, yellow La Poste logo sign (#F5B800 disc), blue signage (#1F3FA0). Grey zinc gutter and downpipe.

### Long ochre building on the square [SEEN, winter photo, flat light]
- 2 storeys, very long, low tile roof with dormer skylights, ochre-buff walls (~#B79A6A), row of blue-lilac shutters (~#8FA0D0), ground-floor central door, yellow La Poste-style sign. Sits beside the church. Probably the same building complex as the post office/school-side but I could not confirm.

### Pharmacie Vert Nature [THUMB, 518x213 image]
- 2-storey rendered building (cream/pink ~#EBD7C9), low hipped tile roof, ground-floor shopfront with dark-green lettering "PHARMACIE" and a green cross (approx #2E9A4B), large glazed door and window bays with green-framed posters. Next door: red-brick house with teal shutters. Wall-mounted blue "handicap" sign visible. Could not verify the cross was lit/animated.

### Chapelle Notre-Dame, Salle des Fêtes, schools, Vival, marketplace [NOT SEEN]
- No Bérat-specific photo found. Bing results returned other towns (generic Vival shops have a green "vival" logo on white/grey; not Bérat-verified).
- The "Salle des fêtes Bérat" result was only a map thumbnail.
- I saw the modern school-like brick single-storey block in a "Terrasses moyennes de la Garonne" Atlas thumbnail: flat/low roof, red brick, big glazed windows (not confirmed as Bérat).
- Recommendation: model the marketplace as an open square (gravel/sand-coloured ground, painted parking bays, tall dark-grey lamp posts with a dish-shade lamp, plane trees planted in rows, white marble war memorial statue on a plinth at the square edge). [SEEN in the winter photo]. Mairie arcade gives the "halle" feel.

## 5. Suggested generator rules
- Roof: hipped (70%) or gable (30%), pitch 20 deg, terracotta palette above, overhang 0.4 m, grey gutter on one eave.
- Walls: pick one of {cream, pink, white render} with brick surround trim on every window and door (mairie/church pattern); ~30% of old buildings full exposed brick with limestone corner blocks.
- Shutters: default light blue (60%), pale grey (15%), teal (10%), roller-shutter dark grey (15%).
- Storeys: houses 1-2, civic buildings 2, mairie-style with 5-arch arcade.
- Church: white octagonal tower 4x nave height, 3 arched tiers with brick bands, balustrade top, brick-buttressed nave, tile roof.
- Fences: brick pier + dark iron railing, or low render wall; green mesh near public sites.
